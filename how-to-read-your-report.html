Why $80,000?

A general manager you'd hire to run the place earns about $75,000. If you're going to do that job yourself, the business should pay you more than the manager you'd otherwise pay — and since the math is after income tax, $80k is real take-home. Clear it and running your own business is worth the effort. Fall short, and you've bought yourself a job.
