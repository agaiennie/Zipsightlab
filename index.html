Questions

The honest answers

How do I get my report — and do I have to wait?

No waiting. You enter your ZIP (and pick a business format where needed), and the report is built right there in your browser and shown on screen. You can download it or save it as a PDF. Nothing is emailed, and no person processes anything.

Where does the data come from — did you make it up?

No. Demographics come live from the US Census (American Community Survey); spending patterns from the BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey; cost structures from published industry benchmarks. Every report prints its sources and its assumptions.

Why do I pick a “format” instead of my exact business?

Our data models cost structure and demand at the format level — a full-service restaurant, a quick-service counter, a drive-through and a food truck have very different economics, but “pizza vs. burgers” doesn’t change the model. Pick the format that matches how you’d actually operate.

Is a human or an AI writing my report?

Neither. Each report is produced by a deterministic model — the same math every time, traceable end to end. We’d rather show you the arithmetic than have you trust a black box.

What’s the “$80k test”?

A general manager you’d hire to run the place earns about $75,000. If you’re doing that job yourself, the business should pay you more — and since the math is after tax, $80,000 take-home is the line that makes doing it yourself worth the effort.

How does payment work?

Checkout is handled by PayPal, and your report unlocks immediately. One honest note: on these low-priced reports the unlock is currently on the honor system — we don’t yet verify each payment server-side (a verified-checkout upgrade is on the roadmap). You’re covered either way by the “useful, or it’s free” guarantee.
